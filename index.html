Oopbuy shopping guide

Turn a saved link into a useful decision.

A long list of links becomes useful when every item answers the same practical questions. Keep the original listing, your comparison notes and the date you checked it together.

Start your research ↗

  1. Choose a category and a purpose

    Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a warm layer, sportswear or an accessory. Compare items that serve the same purpose.

  2. Record measurable details

    Save the item URL, selected variant, listed material, measurements and price with its currency. Write “unknown” when the listing does not provide a detail.

  3. Check photos and purchasing compatibility

    Compare multiple views of the same variant. Before ordering, check whether the purchasing service accepts the original product link and item type.

  4. Compare the full estimated cost

    Include delivery, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Treat unconfirmed figures as estimates.

What belongs in your shortlist?

Product group Record these details Resolve before ordering

Clear answers

Before you open another tab.

Common questions about spreadsheet research, category browsing and product checks.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-research list organized around items shoppers want to investigate for use with Oopbuy. A useful list records product links, categories, measurements and notes. An entry alone does not verify a seller, product authenticity or current availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and research guidance. Its shopping links open CAFANS category pages or the CAFANS homepage.

Can every CAFANS product be purchased through Oopbuy?

Compatibility has not been verified for every listing. Check the original product URL and the purchasing service's current acceptance rules before paying.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you already own. For shoes, compare measured foot length with the listing's size chart. Check units and whether measurements refer to the foot, insole or garment.

Do brand names and photos prove authenticity?

No. A brand name, logo or photograph does not independently establish authenticity. Request reliable provenance and review the seller's product information before making a purchase.

What costs belong in a shopping comparison?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.
